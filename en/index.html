FAQ

What people ask before we start.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the scope. Before we start, your SOW spells out what gets delivered and in which stages, and during the project you can check progress with your client code. For urgent website repairs we aim to respond within 24–48 hours, subject to availability and written confirmation, with a 50% surcharge. Timelines are estimates, not guarantees.

How do payments work?

In stages. Work starts after an initial deposit (25% to 50%, as set in your SOW); progress payments cover work already done and are non-refundable; and the final balance is paid before launch and handoff. You can pay online by card through Stripe. Deposit cancellation and refund terms are in our Terms of Service.

Who owns the code?

You do. Once you've paid 100%, everything custom-built for your project becomes yours and we hand over the repository and access. BDV only keeps its own reusable base libraries and internal tools, and third-party or open-source components stay under their own licenses.

Is there support after delivery?

Delivery doesn't include monthly maintenance, but you can add it: website maintenance from $99 to $249/mo, or an app & platform plan from $299 to $699/mo. Plans are month-to-month and you can cancel anytime, with no penalty. You can also request one-off repairs whenever you need them.

Black Friday & holiday offer, through January 6, 2027: website $79–$199/mo and apps $180–$450/mo. Sign your SOW and pay the deposit by January 6 and keep that maintenance rate for 12 months from the start of your plan (conditions in our Terms of Service).

Do you work with clients outside the U.S.?

Yes. We work remotely, in English and Spanish, with digital SOW signing and online payments in U.S. dollars (USD), so we can review projects from other countries. Agreements are governed by California law. We can't provide services where U.S. sanctions prohibit it. Write to us and we'll look at your case.

What if I want to change something mid-project?

You can. Changes outside the original scope are agreed through a written change order, with its price, before we do them. No surprises on the invoice.
