OUR COMMITMENTS

Scope in writing

What gets built is in your SOW. Any out-of-scope change is agreed in writing before it's done.

5 business days to review

After each delivery you get 5 business days to review it and request fixes within what your SOW covers.

You own the code once paid

Once paid in full, everything custom-built for you becomes yours, with repository and access. Third-party and open-source components stay under their own licenses.

Publishing on the App Store and Google Play

We prepare your app and submit it to the stores, but whether an app is approved and stays in the stores is up to Apple and Google alone. Good to know from the start:

  • The Apple Developer Program membership costs 99 USD a year.[Source 3]
  • Apple says it typically reviews at least 50% of submissions in under 24 hours and 90% in under 48 hours.[Source 4]
  • Google Play charges a one-time registration fee of 25 USD.[Source 5]
  • Personal Google Play accounts created after November 13, 2023 must run a closed test with at least 12 testers for 14 straight days before publishing.[Source 6]

Apple and Google fees are your responsibility unless your SOW says otherwise.

App development FAQ

How much does it cost to build an app with BDV?

Our standard rate starts at $4,900, in milestones. Typical project: $8,000–$15,000 · full MVP from $15,000, quoted. The final price is agreed in writing in your SOW before any work starts.

Black Friday & holiday offer, through January 6, 2027: apps from $2,500 (conditions in our Terms of Service).

Do you build for iPhone and Android at the same time?

Yes. We usually use React Native, which allows one codebase for both platforms. If your project needs something different, we define it in the SOW.

Do you guarantee Apple or Google will approve my app?

No. We prepare the app following their rules and submit it, but whether an app is approved and stays in the stores is up to Apple and Google alone.

How long does it take to build an app?

It depends on the scope. For app projects reviewed on Clutch, the typical timeline is about 11 months; an MVP with fewer features takes less. Before we start, your SOW spells out what gets delivered and in which stages. Timelines are estimates, not guarantees.

Who owns the code?

You do. Once you've paid 100%, everything custom-built for your project becomes yours and we hand over the repository and access. BDV only keeps its own reusable base libraries and internal tools, and third-party or open-source components stay under their own licenses.
